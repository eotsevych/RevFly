How it works

From voice to finished text in one keypress

  1. 1

    Hold your hotkey

    Right ⌥ on a Mac, or any combo like Ctrl+Shift+Space. Release to finish, or tap once to toggle.

  2. 2

    Transcribed on-device

    Silence is trimmed by Silero VAD, then Whisper or Parakeet turns speech into text locally, in memory.

  3. 3

    Translated only if needed

    Already in your target language? Translation is skipped instantly with no network call at all.

  4. 4

    Pasted where you type

    Clean, formatted text lands at your cursor with a simulated ⌘V / Ctrl+V, in any application.

Features

Everything you need to talk instead of type

⌨️

Global hotkey & hold-to-talk

  • Single modifier keys on macOS: Right ⌥ or Right ⌃
  • Any key combo on Windows and Linux
  • Hold-to-talk or tap-to-toggle
  • Esc cancels recording or translation instantly
  • Subtle chimes on start and finish
🧠

On-device speech recognition

  • Whisper models (Medium, Small, Base) via whisper.cpp, multilingual and offline
  • Parakeet TDT 0.6B for near-instant dictation on Apple Silicon
  • Import your own Whisper models
  • Models unload after idle time to free up RAM
〰️

Voice activity detection

  • Silero VAD runs locally on ONNX Runtime
  • Strips silence and long pauses before recognition
  • 400 ms safety margins keep whispers and soft consonants
  • Tunable chunking for long dictation
🌍

Smart AI translation

  • Google Gemini for fast, fluent translation
  • Local LLMs through Ollama or LM Studio
  • Any OpenAI-compatible endpoint with your own prompt template
  • Instant skip for your target language and any languages you exclude
✨

Automatic clean-up

  • Removes "um", "uh", stutters and repeated words
  • Formats numbers, currency, dates, times and URLs
  • Fixes spoken corrections for you

Said"um so I think, sorry, I know it costs like a hundred dollars"

🛡️

Confidentiality guard

  • Masks names and project codenames before text reaches a cloud API
  • Detects emails, phone numbers and card numbers
  • Your choice of replacement, like [CONFIDENTIAL] or ***
💊

Floating voice pill

  • Tiny always-on-top indicator, even over full-screen apps
  • Live equalizer while listening, then transcribing, translating and done
  • Shows the language direction, like Auto → English
  • Drag it anywhere on screen
🗂️

History you control

  • Text only (default), text + Opus audio, or private RAM-only mode
  • Automatic 500 MB cap and 30-day retention
  • Replay recordings and inspect every pipeline stage in Logs
⬆️

Menu bar & auto-updates

  • Switch microphone, output and translation mode from the tray
  • One-click updates with a notification when a new version is out
  • Light, dark and system themes
  • Audio Labs to test and compare recognition settings

Privacy by design

Your voice stays on your computer

Audio is recorded straight into memory and recognized locally. No audio file is saved while you speak, and nothing is uploaded. Only text is ever sent out, and only when you turn on a cloud translation provider. Use a local LLM and nothing leaves your machine at all.

Text only Default

Keeps transcripts in a local SQLite database. No audio is stored.

Text + audio

Also keeps compact Opus recordings, so you can replay what you said.

Private mode

Everything stays in RAM. Nothing is written to disk, ever.

Settings

Tune every step, or never open it at all

Sensible defaults out of the box. When you want control: General, Theme, Translations, Storage, Logs and Audio Labs.

FAQ

Questions

Is RevFly really free?

Yes. It's open source under the MIT license, with no account, subscription or usage limits. Cloud translation with Gemini uses your own API key; local models cost nothing.

Does my audio ever leave my computer?

No. Recording and speech recognition happen locally, in memory. If you enable cloud translation, only the recognized text is sent, after the confidentiality guard has masked anything sensitive. With a local LLM, nothing leaves your machine.

Which languages does it understand?

Whisper models recognize dozens of languages offline. Translation works between any languages your chosen provider supports, and speech that's already in your target language is pasted as-is without translating.

Why does my computer warn me when I open it?

RevFly isn't signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft certificate yet, so macOS and Windows show a one-time warning for downloads from a browser. The Terminal one-liner and Homebrew avoid it on Mac; the install guide shows how to allow it otherwise. Updates install from inside the app without the warning.

Which apps does it work with?

Any app with a text cursor: Slack, Telegram, WhatsApp, VS Code, Google Docs, Chrome, Terminal and more. RevFly pastes with the normal ⌘V / Ctrl+V shortcut. On Linux, an X11 session works best.
