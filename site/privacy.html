RevFly

Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

RevFly recognizes your speech on your own computer. It has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and its developer receives none of your audio, text or settings. Your text leaves your computer only if you turn on translation, and then only to the translation service you choose.

This policy covers the RevFly desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux, wherever you got it (the Microsoft Store, GitHub, Homebrew or this website). RevFly is free, open-source software published by Yevhenii Otsevich; you can check everything below in its source code.

Your voice

  • RevFly uses the microphone only while you are recording: from the moment you press its hotkey until you finish or cancel.
  • Speech recognition runs entirely on your computer. Audio is never uploaded to the developer or to any service.
  • Whether recordings are kept depends on Settings → Storage: by default only the text is kept; you can choose to also keep the recordings, or to keep nothing at all.

What is stored on your computer

  • Your dictation history (the recognized and translated text, the detected language and the time) and, if you chose to keep them, the recordings.
  • Your settings, including any API keys you enter for a translation service, and diagnostic logs of recent dictations, which contain their text and timings.
  • All of this stays in RevFly's folder on your computer. History is deleted automatically after the period you set in Settings → Storage (30 days by default), and you can clear it or the logs at any time. Uninstalling RevFly from the Microsoft Store removes this folder.

When RevFly connects to the internet

  • Translation, only if you turn it on. The recognized text (never the audio) is sent to the service you select: Google Gemini, OpenAI, a custom service you configure, or a local model on your own computer (such as Ollama or LM Studio), in which case nothing leaves your computer. These services handle the text under their own privacy policies. Before sending, RevFly can mask email addresses, phone numbers, card numbers and words you list (Settings → General → Confidential Text & Masking).
  • Speech models. RevFly downloads the speech recognition model you choose from Hugging Face the first time it is needed. Nothing about you is sent beyond the ordinary details of a download request, such as your IP address.
  • Updates. Versions installed from GitHub, Homebrew or this website check GitHub once a day for a new release. The Microsoft Store version gets updates from the Store instead and makes no such check.

What RevFly does not do

  • It does not collect analytics, usage statistics or crash reports.
  • It does not show ads or share or sell any data.
  • It does not require an account.

Clipboard and pasting

To paste your text, RevFly places it on the clipboard and presses paste in the app you are typing in. It does not read your clipboard or what is on screen.

Children

RevFly is a general-purpose tool. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Its history is kept in the project's public repository.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/eotsevych/RevFly/issues.
